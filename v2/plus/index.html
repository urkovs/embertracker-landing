Good to know.

When will Ember Plus be available?

Plus is coming soon. Availability will be posted after the public app and supported services are verified.

Can I use Ember without Plus?

Yes. Daily logging, Calendar, treatment history, core reports, and manual backup stay free.

Does Plus predict migraine attacks?

No. Planned tools show observations and descriptive comparisons. They do not predict attacks or prove that one factor caused them.

What do weather and health comparisons need?

You choose the permissions and sources. Comparisons appear only when enough recorded days and supported readings are available; missing readings stay missing.

Where are my records stored?

Core health records are stored on your device. Optional automatic backups are encrypted before leaving your phone. Free manual backup stays separate, and you can recover an existing automatic backup after Plus access ends.

Questions about Plus.

When will Ember Plus be available?

Plus is coming soon. A release date has not been announced.

Does Plus predict migraine attacks?

No. It compares recorded observations. A comparison does not establish a cause.

What do weather and Health comparisons need?

You choose the sources and permissions. Comparisons need enough recorded days and supported readings; missing data stays missing.

Where are my records stored?

Health records stay on your device unless you choose to back up or share them. Automatic backups are encrypted before leaving your phone, and recovery remains available after Plus ends.

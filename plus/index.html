A few things to know.

When can I get Ember Plus?

Ember Plus is coming soon. Launch details and pricing will be shared when it is ready.

Do I need Plus to use Ember?

No. Daily logging, medication and treatment tracking, provider reports, and manual backups remain free.

Do I have to connect a wearable?

No. Each connection is optional. Health imports depend on what your device or other apps make available through Apple Health or Health Connect.

Does Plus predict migraine attacks?

No. Weather and personal comparisons offer context for your records. They do not diagnose migraine, establish a cause, or replace care from your clinician.

What changes about where my records are stored?

The free app stores your health records on your phone. Ember Plus will include optional encrypted backup; turning it on stores an encrypted copy remotely for recovery. Weather and health connections are also optional.

Before you start

Questions about Ember Plus.

How do I get Ember Plus?

In Ember, open the Discover tab and tap See Ember Plus. Then tap See plans to choose yearly or monthly.

How does the free trial work?

Every Plus feature is free for 14 days, and Ember reminds you 2 days before the trial ends if notifications are on. Cancel in App Store or Google Play settings before then and you pay nothing.

Does Ember Plus predict migraines?

No. Ember Plus shows patterns in your own records. Pressure alerts describe the weather forecast, and they mention when a change matches one of your patterns.

Does Ember Plus work on Android?

Yes. Plus imports from Health Connect on Android and Apple Health on iPhone. A wearable is optional, and Apple Watch logging needs an iPhone.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep the free app and every record on your phone. Plus features stop when your plan ends, and you can still restore an existing backup.

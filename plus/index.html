Good to know.

When can I get Ember Plus?

Plus is coming soon. The features above preview what’s planned.

Do I need a wearable?

No. Wearables are optional. Apple Watch logging requires an iPhone and Apple Watch.

Does Plus predict migraine attacks?

No. It shows patterns in your records, not predictions or proof of a cause.

Where are my records stored?

Your diary stays on your phone. Enabling automatic backup also stores an encrypted copy remotely.

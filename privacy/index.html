Ember Migraine Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

The core diary is stored on your device. Optional automatic backup sends an encrypted copy to Google Cloud. Account, purchase, weather, diagnostic and support services handle the information described below.

Ember is operated by Cascade Headache, PLLC. This policy covers Ember and this website. Feature availability varies by app version and account; describing an optional or testing feature here does not enable it or make it available to everyone.

For Washington consumer health data, also read our Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice.

1. Diary and connected health information

Ember stores the information you enter on your device: headache and migraine symptoms, severity, impact, notes, medications, treatments, menstrual-cycle information, questionnaire responses, profile details and provider or pharmacy details. It uses those records to provide tracking, observations and reports.

If you connect Apple Health or Health Connect, Ember imports supported readings you authorize, such as sleep, activity and vital-sign readings, for on-device features. Imported raw Health readings are excluded from Ember backups. Saved personal experiments can contain results derived from those readings and can be included in an encrypted backup.

2. Optional encrypted backup

When you turn on automatic backup, Ember encrypts eligible records on your device and sends the encrypted copy to Google Cloud storage in the United States. The service also handles account-linked identifiers, backup versions, timestamps and security information needed to operate and protect the backup. This metadata is not anonymous.

The backup service does not receive the recovery key needed to read your encrypted records. Recovery material stays in secure storage on your device and, depending on your choice and platform support, may also be kept through iCloud Keychain, Google Block Store, or a recovery code you save yourself. Restoring through Ember requires account access and the applicable recovery material. If the recovery material is lost, we cannot reconstruct it from your account password.

Turning backup off, deleting a backup, deleting your account and uninstalling the app are different actions. Uninstalling does not request remote deletion. Backup settings and the account-deletion process provide the relevant controls; contact us if a deletion fails. Never send us your recovery code.

3. Accounts and purchases

Firebase Authentication, provided by Google, processes your email address or Apple Sign-In identifier and authentication information to provide sign-in and account protection. Diary records are not uploaded to Firebase Authentication as part of sign-in.

Apple or Google and RevenueCat process purchase, receipt, account and entitlement information for optional purchases and access management. We do not receive your full payment-card details. Core tracking remains free; Ember Plus is not currently offered for public purchase.

4. Weather and approximate location

If you use weather features, Ember sends your selected city or approximate coordinates, with the relevant device permission, through Ember's weather relay to Visual Crossing. Weather requests do not include your diary or medication records. You can choose a city instead of device location and change location permission in device settings.

5. Diagnostics, website and support

Ember uses Sentry for error diagnostics to find and fix software problems. Diagnostic information can include error details, code locations, app and device information, and technical connection metadata. We apply collection and redaction controls; the fields depend on the installed app version. We do not describe these records as anonymous or use them for advertising.

Our website hosting and other network providers receive technical connection information when you visit or use their services. The website loads fonts from Google Fonts. The contact page opens your email app to write to our Google Workspace mailbox. If you email us, we receive what you include. We use support messages to respond and handle requests. Do not send detailed health records, urgent medical concerns or recovery codes through ordinary support email.

6. Recipients and sharing

The services described above include Google Cloud, Firebase, Google Workspace, Apple, Google Play, RevenueCat, Sentry, Visual Crossing and our website host, GitHub Pages. They receive information for the functions described here. Google Cloud receives encrypted eligible backup records and backup metadata; Firebase receives authentication information; Apple, Google and RevenueCat receive purchase or access-management information; Sentry receives diagnostic information; Visual Crossing receives weather-request location information; and Google Workspace receives support messages you send. iCloud Keychain or Google Block Store may receive recovery material separately from the Google Cloud backup ciphertext.

You choose the recipient when you export or share a report or file. A shared PDF or JSON export is not protected by Ember's automatic-backup encryption; its recipient and the sharing service handle it under their own practices. Direct clinic-email delivery is not currently available in the public app.

We do not sell personal or consumer health data or share it for targeted advertising. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect security and legal rights, subject to applicable law. We do not use geofencing around healthcare facilities to identify people seeking care or deliver health-related advertising.

We do not share personal information with other businesses under common ownership or control. Reports you choose to send to your clinician are handled separately.

7. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you edit, delete, reset or restore them, or your device removes them. Your own exports and recipients' copies are separate.

Automatic backup keeps up to ten verified versions, with cleanup of older versions; failed cleanup can temporarily retain additional versions. There is no automatic deletion solely because Plus access expires. Successful remote-backup deletion removes stored encrypted versions. Minimal revocation markers may remain to prevent previously enrolled devices from recreating a deleted backup. Account deletion also removes the authentication account through the app's deletion process.

Account information is retained while the account is active. Support, security, diagnostic and purchase records have separate purposes and retention requirements. We retain them only as needed for those purposes, applicable legal obligations and documented exceptions.

Using the in-app account-deletion control removes the authentication account and local Ember records after the supported backup-deletion step succeeds. It does not itself delete store transaction records, RevenueCat customer records, Sentry diagnostic records, or support email. For a request about those records, use the privacy-request process below; we will explain the records involved, any retention exception, and the outcome. Copies retained by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, Sentry, Google Workspace, Visual Crossing or another recipient follow that recipient's applicable process and retention rules.

See Delete your Ember account for in-app and email options. Deleting an account does not cancel a store subscription; cancel through Apple or Google separately.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You control what you enter, connected-health permissions, weather choices, automatic backup, exports and sharing. You can edit or delete local records and use export tools. Turning a feature off stops its future use but may not erase previously stored information.

For access, correction, deletion, portability, consent withdrawal, restriction or objection requests where applicable, email hello@cascadeheadache.com. Sam handles requests for Cascade Headache, PLLC. Tell us what you want to do and the account email; do not include your diary or recovery code. We may need proportionate verification of account ownership. You do not need to create a new account to make a request.

We respond within the deadlines that apply to your request and explain any lawful extension or exception. To appeal a refusal, reply with “Privacy request appeal” in the subject; we will reconsider and explain the outcome and available complaint route. You may also contact your applicable privacy regulator. See the Washington notice for its specific rights and appeal process.

9. International use

Cascade Headache, PLLC operates in the United States. Networked services may process information in the United States or other countries where providers operate. These countries may have different privacy laws. We assess applicable transfer safeguards and contractual requirements for those services. Local device processing or encryption does not, by itself, remove privacy rights or make every jurisdiction's law inapplicable.

10. Security and clinical care

Safeguards include device storage controls, account authentication, encrypted automatic-backup content and service access controls. No system can guarantee complete security. Protect your device, account and recovery material.

Ember is a consumer tracking app. Using it does not create a clinician-patient relationship. Cascade also provides clinical care, but it does not retrieve your Ember diary for clinical care; clinicians receive reports you choose to share. A clinician's handling of a received report is subject to that clinician's practices and applicable duties. We make no HIPAA-certification claim or blanket legal exemption based on local storage or encryption.

11. Age audience

Ember's current terms specify a minimum age of 17. If you believe a child has provided information through Ember, contact us so we can assess and address it. Local deletion alone does not remove information already sent to a network service.

12. Policy changes and contact

We update this policy when practices change and identify the update date. We provide additional notice and obtain affirmative consent where required before a new collection, sharing or use. Continued use alone does not replace a legally required consent.

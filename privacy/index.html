Ember Migraine Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

The short version: Your health data stays on your device. We do not collect it, transmit it, or sell it.

1. Information We Collect

Authentication Data

When you create an account, Ember collects your email address through Firebase Authentication for the sole purpose of managing your login credentials. If you sign in with Apple, we receive only the identifiers Apple provides. No health data is stored in your account.

Optional Support Purchase Data

If you choose to support Ember through an optional in-app purchase, RevenueCat processes the purchase through Apple's App Store or Google Play. We receive only your support status and purchase receipts. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Website Contact Messages

If you submit a website contact form or email us, we receive the name, email address, audience type, topic, and message you choose to provide. Contact forms are forwarded to hello@cascadeheadache.com through FormSubmit. Please do not include urgent medical concerns or detailed personal health information in website contact messages.

Health Data You Enter

Ember allows you to record daily headache and migraine symptoms, severity, and functional impact; medications taken; menstrual cycle data; treatment history and injection dates; disability questionnaire responses; baseline frequency estimates; and provider and pharmacy information.

All health data is stored locally on your device only. It is never transmitted to our servers, to third parties, or to any cloud service.

Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect your location data. We do not use behavioral analytics platforms, tracking pixels, or advertising identifiers. We do not use cookies, session replay, or similar tracking technologies. We do not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA) or behavioral usage telemetry.

Crash Reports

Ember uses Sentry, a crash reporting service, to capture stack traces when the app encounters an unexpected error. Crash reports include the location of the error in our code (file name, line number), your device's operating system version, and basic device model information.

Crash reports are configured to explicitly exclude your name, email, or any other identifying information; any health data you have entered; your IP address or device-level identifiers (IDFV, IDFA); and any screen recordings or session replays. This data is used solely to identify and fix bugs in the app.

Optional Data Sharing

If you choose to use the export features, Ember generates a PDF report or chart note that you can share via your device's standard sharing functionality. This sharing is initiated and controlled entirely by you. We have no access to shared reports.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

All health data entered into Ember is stored locally on your device using your device's built-in storage system. No health data is transmitted to external servers.

Because your health data exists only on your device:

  • If you delete the app, your health data is permanently deleted.
  • If you use the "Delete all my data" feature within the app, all data is permanently and irreversibly removed.
  • If you lose or replace your device without a device backup, your data cannot be recovered.
  • We cannot access, retrieve, or restore your health data under any circumstances.

We recommend using Ember's backup export if you wish to preserve your data across device changes.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your health data never leaves your device, so we never see it, process it, or use it. The app processes your data entirely on your device to provide its core features: automatic classification of headache and migraine days, report generation, medication tracking, treatment response calculations, and clinical chart note generation.

Your account email address is used solely for authentication purposes.

If you contact us through the website or by email, we use the information you provide to respond to your message, understand feedback, and improve Ember.

4. Third-Party Services

Ember uses these third-party services. None receive app health logs:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google): Manages your login credentials only. Receives your email address or Apple Sign-In identifier. Does not receive any health data.
  • RevenueCat: Manages optional support status and purchase receipts only. Does not receive any health data.
  • Sentry: Receives anonymized crash reports (stack traces, operating system version, device model) when the app encounters an error. Configured to exclude all user identifiers and all health data. See "Crash Reports" in Section 1 above.
  • FormSubmit: Forwards website contact form submissions to us. Receives only the contact form fields you choose to submit.

Ember does not integrate with behavioral analytics platforms, advertising networks, cloud databases of health information, or social media services.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your health data to any third party because we do not have access to your health data. The only way your health data leaves your device is if you actively choose to share it using the app's export features.

Website contact messages are separate from app health logs. They are sent to us only when you submit a form or email us, and we use them to respond to you.

6. Children's Privacy

Ember is not directed to children under 17 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 17. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the app, please note that all health data is stored locally on the device and can be deleted using the "Delete all my data" feature.

7. HIPAA and Health Data

Ember is designed to assist patients in tracking their own health information. Because the app stores health data exclusively on your personal device and does not transmit health data to any server or third party, the app itself is not a "covered entity" or "business associate" under HIPAA.

The app is designed with privacy principles consistent with HIPAA's minimum necessary standard: no unnecessary data collection, local-only storage, patient-controlled data sharing, and complete data deletion capability.

If you share your data with your healthcare provider, that communication is subject to your provider's own privacy practices.

8. Data Security

Your health data is protected by your device's built-in security features, including its passcode or biometric lock, device encryption, and operating system app sandboxing. Because no health data is transmitted over the internet, there is no risk of interception during transmission.

9. Your Rights and Choices

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All data you enter is visible to you within the app at all times.
  • Export: You can export your data as a PDF report or chart note at any time.
  • Deletion: You can permanently delete all your data using the "Delete all my data" feature. This action is irreversible.
  • Modification: You can edit your entries, baselines, and settings at any time.
  • Account: You can delete your account, which removes your authentication credentials from Firebase.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. Your continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

11. California Residents (CCPA)

Because Ember does not collect, store, or transmit personal health information to any server or third party, the categories of information typically disclosed under the California Consumer Privacy Act do not apply. We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. The personal data we may hold is limited to authentication email, optional support receipt status, and information you voluntarily submit through email or website contact forms.

12. European Users (GDPR)

Because Ember stores all health data locally on your device and does not transmit health data to servers located anywhere, no personal health data is processed by us as a data controller or data processor. You retain complete control of your health data at all times. The app's local-only architecture means that GDPR data subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability) are exercised directly through the app's built-in features. For your authentication data (email), you may request deletion by deleting your account within the app. For website contact messages, email us to request deletion.

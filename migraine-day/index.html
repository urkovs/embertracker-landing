Migraine days

What counts as a migraine day?

In Ember, it’s a day with head pain and 2 or more migraine features, or symptoms that limit your day. Researchers haven’t settled on one definition yet.

Day types

Every day is one of four types.

Migraine day
Head pain with two or more migraine features, symptoms without head pain that limit activity, or a migraine-specific medication logged when pain or symptoms are present.
Headache day
Head pain that doesn’t meet migraine criteria.
Symptom day
Symptoms without head pain or significant impact.
Clear day
No pain, no symptoms.

Migraine features

What counts as a migraine feature.

Each feature counts once, and nausea and vomiting count together as one. Brain fog and fatigue are tracked but don’t count.

What counts as a migraine feature

  • Moderate or severe head pain
  • Functional limitation
  • Light sensitivity
  • Sound sensitivity
  • Aura
  • Smell sensitivity
  • Neck pain
  • Dizziness
  • Nausea or vomiting

Tracked but not counted as features

  • Brain fog
  • Fatigue

Medicine

A migraine medicine can make it a migraine day.

Logging a migraine-specific medicine on a day with pain or symptoms makes it a migraine day. General pain relievers like ibuprofen and acetaminophen don’t count on their own, because they treat many conditions.

Examples

  • Imitrex
  • Maxalt
  • Zomig
  • Relpax
  • Ubrelvy
  • Nurtec ODT
  • Zavzpret
  • Migranal
  • Trudhesa

Why it matters

Your monthly count guides treatment.

Clinicians use migraine and headache days per month to decide on preventive treatment and to see whether it’s working.3

Research

Researchers are still agreeing on a definition.

Trials of newer migraine preventives used 12 different definitions of a migraine day. A 2026 expert consensus proposed one for the next edition of ICHD.2

It uses how long the headache lasted. Ember doesn’t ask, so its rules leave duration out.

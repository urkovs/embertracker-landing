# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who live with migraine and want a clear, low-effort way to track their days, plus headache clinicians who need concise, clinically useful reports. Utility routes also serve existing Ember patients completing a specific task, such as clinic check-in.

## Product Purpose

Explain Ember clearly, show the real product, and help patients move into the app or share an accurate report with their clinician. Success means visitors understand the app quickly and can complete the next step without uncertainty.

## Brand Personality

Plain, confident, neutral-warm. Clinically grounded without sounding institutional or promotional.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense feature catalogs, folksy flourishes, manufactured idioms, vague clinical claims, invented proof, testimonial marketing, and language that suggests AI interpretation. Do not imitate generic healthcare marketing or hide the real app behind decorative concepts.

## Design Principles

- Say the clinical thing directly.
- Let real product visuals carry the personality.
- Keep patient tasks short and obvious.
- Preserve privacy and product boundaries precisely.
- Use credibility without personal promotion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast in Ember's dark theme, keyboard-visible focus states, responsive layouts down to phone widths, reduced-motion support, and labels that do not depend on color alone.

What arrives

One email with one PDF.

  • Page one is a short summary and a chart note.
  • The calendar follows, for up to the last 90 days.
  • The report has the patient’s first and last name but no date of birth, so confirm identity with your usual checks.
  • Ember doesn’t connect to your EHR. Staff save the PDF to the chart like any outside record.

Setup

Four steps to set up.

  1. Request setup

    Use the form below. Name the inbox that should receive reports.

  2. Confirm your inbox

    We check it with you and send a test report.

  3. Add a payment method

    Your 90 free days start then. Nothing is charged until they end.

  4. Post your QR code

    We add your clinic within 5 business days. The code opens Ember with your clinic selected.

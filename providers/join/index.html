Direct Send

How Direct Send works.

Direct Send adds your clinic to Ember by name. When a patient picks your clinic, Ember prepares their report and supplies your address to their own email app.

Sending is email from the patient’s own account. The report doesn’t pass through Ember’s servers, so Ember’s team never sees it.

What arrives

One email with one PDF.

The email

The subject line names the patient and the date. The body holds a one-line summary of their day counts and acute medication use.

The PDF

Page one is a short summary and a chart note. The patient’s calendar for up to the last 90 days follows in black and white.

Matching the chart

The report carries the patient’s first and last name. It has no date of birth, so confirm identity with your usual checks.

Your EHR

Ember doesn’t connect to your EHR. Staff save the PDF to the chart like any outside record.

Patient control

Nothing sends without the patient.

Patients review the report on their phone first. Nothing leaves the phone until they tap Send in their own email app.

They choose the recipient each time. Without setup, they can still email the report to any provider.

Setup

Four steps to set up.

  1. Request setup with the form below. Name the inbox that should receive reports.
  2. We confirm the inbox with you and send a test report.
  3. We add your clinic to Ember by name. This usually happens the same day, and always within 5 business days.
  4. Post your QR code in the lobby and add your link to after-visit summaries. Scanning the code opens Ember with your clinic selected.

Your inbox

Use an inbox your clinic approves for patient health information. Reports arrive as standard email from the patient’s own account.

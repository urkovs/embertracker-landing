Where the data comes from

Quick daily check-ins take 10 to 20 seconds.

Severity, symptoms, medications, and impact are tap targets on one screen. Ember classifies each day from what was logged, based on programmed ICHD-3 criteria, and displays its reasoning.

Reporting compliance is printed on the chart note output, so you know how much weight the data deserves before you read further.

Safety flags

Nonjudgmental warning flags.

Acute days are counted per medication against overuse thresholds. Patients see a gentle nudge first, then a firmer warning, and the chart note carries a risk-flag section when a month crosses the line.

Attacks with unilateral weakness are called out in the generated chart note as well.

Treatment response

Baselines are captured before treatment starts.

When a patient begins any preventive treatment, Ember captures the baseline first, before it takes effect. For patients on multiple preventives, it reports the response each one has on its own, and combined.

That can help you make the case to payers for reauthorizations or prior authorizations, or give you the data to make clinically sound decisions about switching or discontinuing a regimen.

It reports what they logged. Nothing more.

Health data lives on the patient's phone, with no cloud database behind the app. Every value on the report traces to a dated entry, with no AI and no inferences.

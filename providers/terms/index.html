1. About these terms

These Direct Send terms (the “Clinic Terms”) apply to any clinic, practice or health system (“you”) that subscribes to Ember Direct Send from Cascade Headache, PLLC (“we” or “us”), the developer of Ember.

By starting a free trial or a paid subscription, you agree to these Clinic Terms. The person who accepts them confirms that they are authorized to do so for the clinic.

Back to contents

2. What Direct Send includes

Direct Send lists your clinic by name in Ember and gives you a lobby QR code and a link for after-visit summaries. When a patient chooses your clinic, Ember supplies your report inbox address to the patient’s own email app.

Patients send reports by email from their own accounts. We do not receive, store or forward those reports, and Direct Send does not connect to your electronic health record.

Each subscription covers one clinic listing with one report inbox.

3. Free trial

Each clinic may receive one free trial of 90 days. A card or bank account is required to start it, and the trial begins when you complete checkout.

We send a reminder before the trial ends. If you cancel before the trial ends, you will not be charged.

4. Fees and billing

After the trial, Direct Send costs $50 a month or $500 a year per clinic, plus any applicable taxes. Fees are charged automatically at the start of each billing period to the payment method on file until you cancel.

Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store full card or bank account numbers. Patients never pay for Direct Send.

5. Cancellation and refunds

You may cancel at any time using the billing link we send you or by emailing hello@cascadeheadache.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and your clinic stays listed until then.

Fees already paid are not refundable, including for partial billing periods, except where the law requires a refund.

6. Failed payments

If a payment fails, we will retry it and email the billing contact on file. If payment still fails after the retry period, your subscription ends and we remove your clinic from Ember.

Back to contents

7. Price changes

We will email you at least 30 days before a price change takes effect. A new price applies from your next billing period after that notice.

8. When a subscription ends

When your subscription ends for any reason, we remove your clinic from Ember within 5 business days. Your QR code and link then stop opening your clinic in Ember.

Patients can still email their reports to you by entering your address themselves. Their records stay on their own phones.

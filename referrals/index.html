Some people who share their life with migraine online partner with Ember. If one of them gives you their creator code, you can use it in Ember for a free month of Plus, then 10% off your first payment.

1. The offer

  • A free month of Ember Plus, then 10% off your first payment. In the U.S. that’s $53.99 for the first year of annual Plus (then $59.99 a year), or $7.19 for the first month of monthly Plus (then $7.99 a month), before any tax.
  • The App Store or Google Play shows the exact price and renewal terms before you confirm. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel in your Apple or Google account.
  • Ember’s core tracking stays free. You never need a code to use Ember.
Back to contents

2. Who can use a code

  • You enter the code in Ember before your first paid Plus purchase, then buy within 60 days.
  • One creator code per account. Once chosen, it can’t be changed.
  • People who have paid for Plus before, restored or transferred a past Plus purchase, or received Plus through Family Sharing without buying it themselves aren’t eligible. Free users, past Support Ember supporters and people who only tried a free trial or preview are eligible.
  • A store free trial or preview doesn’t use up the offer.
Back to contents

3. What the creator gets

The creator earns a commission from Ember when you subscribe with their code. They see totals only, never your name, your account or anything you log.

Back to contents

4. Your privacy

Using a code sends your Ember account identifier, the code you chose and your Plus purchase details to Ember’s referral service, so the commission can be calculated. Your health info lives on your phone and is never part of this. If you delete your Ember account, your referral records are erased; Ember keeps only the unlinked financial records the law requires. Details are in the privacy policy.

Back to contents

5. Fair use

No self-referrals, duplicate accounts or fabricated purchases. Ember may decline or reverse a referral that breaks these terms, without affecting your Plus subscription or your health records.

Back to contents

6. Changes and questions

Ember may change or close creator codes for future purchases with at least 30 days’ notice. A code you’ve already used keeps the terms that applied when you used it. For a question or a disputed referral, email hello@cascadeheadache.com. Please don’t email health records, tax forms or banking details.

What you receive

The numbers you ask for at every follow-up.

90-day calendar

Day type, impact, acute medications and menstrual days for every date, with treatment starts on the day they happened.

Chart note

Monthly migraine and headache days, MIDAS, acute medication days and response to each preventive. Patients paste it into your portal.

Safety flags

Acute medication days counted by class against standard overuse thresholds. Attacks with one-sided weakness are called out.

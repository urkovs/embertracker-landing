Questions from clinicians.

Who can see a patient’s report?

Only the people the patient sends it to. A report emailed or shared from the patient’s phone doesn’t pass through Ember’s servers, so Ember’s team never receives it.

Does Ember connect to our EHR?

No. The report is a PDF your staff can attach to the chart. Patients can also copy the chart note as plain text and send it through your patient portal.

Does a patient need Ember Plus?

No. Logging and the report are free to use. Ember Plus is an optional subscription, and the report doesn’t need it.

What if a patient logs only some days?

The note says so in its coverage line, which separates logged days from presumed-clear and unknown ones. Unlogged days count as clear only if the patient chose that setting, and the calendar marks missed days with a dash.

Can I change a patient’s medication limits?

Yes. Set each acute class’s monthly limit in Provider Handoff. After the patient imports the plan, Ember’s overuse warnings use your limits.

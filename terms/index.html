Ember is a personal health tracking application that helps users log headache and migraine symptoms, track medication usage, and generate reports summarizing their headache patterns. The App is designed to support communication between patients and their healthcare providers.

3. Medical disclaimer

The App is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or condition.

The App provides automated classifications (such as “migraine day” or “headache day”) based on the symptom information you enter and published clinical criteria. These classifications are intended solely to assist in organizing your health data for discussion with your healthcare provider. They do not constitute a medical diagnosis.

The App may generate reports, chart notes, or clinical summaries. These outputs are informational tools designed to support, not replace, the clinical judgment of your healthcare provider.

You should:

  • Always consult your healthcare provider before making any changes to your treatment plan.
  • Not rely on the App as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
  • Seek immediate medical attention if you experience a severe or unusual headache, sudden onset of the worst headache of your life, or any neurological emergency.

The information provided by the App about medication overuse risk, treatment response percentages, and disability scoring is for informational purposes only. Your healthcare provider is the appropriate person to interpret this data in the context of your individual medical history.
